# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-23

AlarmingNotifications is free and open-source software. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share user data.
The app also does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or
third-party services that collect user data.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not collect or transmit user data, the developer does not retain any user data and has no user data to delete.

## Third Parties

The app does not share user data with third parties (because it has none to share).

## Permissions Used

- `android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`: needed to see notification contents to know when to trigger an alarm.
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: needed to post the alarming notification.
- `android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM`: needed to support snoozing of alarms.

No other permissions are requested or used.
